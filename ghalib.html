Aa gaye? Achha hua. Warna raat kaise katit?

Mirza Ghalib

1797 – 1869 · Urdu & Persian Poet · Delhi

Mirza Ghalib wrote as if wit and ruin were roommates — no poet has ever sounded so amused by heartbreak while still bleeding from it.

ہزاروں خواہشیں ایسی کہ ہر خواہش پہ دم نکلے

Hazaaron khwahishen aisi ke har khwahish pe dam nikle

Thousands of desires, each worth dying for. Many were fulfilled — yet too few.

ہم کو معلوم ہے جنت کی حقیقت لیکن

Hum ko maloom hai jannat ki haqeeqat lekin, Dil ke khush rakhne ko Ghalib yeh khayaal achha hai

I know the truth about paradise — but sometimes a beautiful lie keeps the heart alive.

دل ہی تو ہے نہ سنگ و خشت، درد سے بھر نہ آئے کیوں

Dil hi toh hai na sang-o-khisht, dard se bhar na aaye kyun

It is only a heart, not brick or stone — why shouldn’t it fill with pain?

رگوں میں دوڑتے پھرنے کے ہم نہیں قائل

Ragon mein daudte phirne ke hum nahin qaayil, Jab aankh hi se na tapka toh phir lahu kya hai

I do not care for blood that merely runs through veins — if it cannot rise to the eyes, what kind of blood is it?

قیدِ حیات و بندِ غم، اصل میں دونوں ایک ہیں

Qaid-e-hayaat o band-e-gham, asl mein dono ek hain

The prison of life and the prison of sorrow are, in truth, the same. Why should a man expect freedom from grief before death?

اگر میری غزلیں کسی اجنبی کی رات خراب کر رہی ہیں، تو سمجھو میں اب بھی زندہ ہوں۔

Agar meri ghazlein kisi ajnabi ki raat kharaab kar rahi hain, toh samjho main ab bhi zinda hoon.

If my verses are still ruining a stranger’s night somewhere, then I suppose I am still alive.
