Itni door aaye ho. Baitho.

Javed Akhtar

1945 – Present · Poet · Lyricist · Screenwriter · Mumbai

Javed Akhtar wrote like someone who had spent years arguing with the world, only to discover the quieter battle was always with himself.

ये तेरा घर, ये मेरा घर किसी को देखना हो गर तो पहले आके माँग ले मेरी नज़र, तेरी नज़र।

Yeh tera ghar, yeh mera ghar, Kisi ko dekhna ho gar, To pehle aake maang le, Meri nazar, teri nazar.

This is your home, this is mine too. If anyone wishes to truly see it, they must first borrow your eyes, or mine.

हम तो बचपन में भी अकेले थे सिर्फ़ दिल की गली में खेले थे।

Hum toh bachpan mein bhi akele the, Sirf dil ki gali mein khele the.

Even in childhood, I was alone. I played only in the narrow streets of my own heart.

कभी किसी को मुकम्मल जहाँ नहीं मिलता कहीं ज़मीन तो कहीं आसमाँ नहीं मिलता।

Kabhi kisi ko mukammal jahaan nahin milta, Kahin zameen toh kahin aasmaan nahin milta.

No one is ever given a complete world. Some are missing the earth, some the sky.

मैं कब से कितना हूँ तन्हा तुझे पता भी नहीं

Main kab se kitna hoon tanha, tujhe pata bhi nahin

You don’t even know how long I’ve been lonely. You at least have a god. I don’t even have that.

ज़रा चेहरों की सच्चाई पे गौर कर लेना

Zara chehron ki sachchai pe gaur kar lena, Yeh log jitne dikhte hain, utne achhe nahin.

Look carefully at the honesty of faces. People are rarely as kind as they appear.

अगर तुम यहाँ तक पहुँच ही गए हो, तो बैठो।

Agar tum yahaan tak pahunch hi gaye ho, toh baitho. Itni door koi sirf galti se nahin aata.

If you’ve made it this far, sit down. Nobody comes this far by accident.
